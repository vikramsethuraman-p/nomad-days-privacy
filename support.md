## About Nomad Days

Nomad Days helps international travelers automatically track their travel history, visa stay limits, tax residency thresholds, and Foreign Earned Income Exclusion (FEIE) eligibility.

## Privacy

Nomad Days is designed with privacy in mind. Your personal travel history is stored on your device, and no account is required.
